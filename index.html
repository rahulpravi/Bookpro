About Me

Hello! I’m Hannah, an accomplished author and expert in Amazon KDP publishing. Through years of hands-on experience, I've helped countless authors turn their stories into successful, self-published books. My passion lies in guiding aspiring writers from manuscript to marketplace.

Whether you’re a seasoned writer or just starting out, my mission is to empower you — to create, publish, and thrive on a global scale. I’ll help you navigate KDP, increase your book's visibility, and maximize your potential for success.

Every author has a unique voice. Let’s make sure yours is heard, and your book reaches its full potential.

My Services

Book Formatting

Professional formatting for Amazon KDP, B&N Press, Apple Books, Google Play & more. eBook, paperback, or both – I'll transform your manuscript into a clean, publish-ready file meeting all industry standards.

Mock Up Cover Design

Eye-catching, custom book covers reflecting your story's essence. Work with you from concept to completion for a cover that stands out and supports your book's success.

Marketing

Tailored marketing strategies to increase visibility, attract your target audience, and drive consistent sales. Listing optimization, social media, email campaigns, ad campaigns, and more.

Book Cover Design

Custom, professional paperback covers tailored to your genre and platform. High-res, print-ready designs formatted to KDP specs, with impeccable typography and bestseller appeal.

Amazon KDP Publishing

End-to-end publishing on Amazon KDP and major platforms. Formatting, metadata, final upload – I handle it all for a seamless, confident book launch.

Marketing Plan & Crowdfunding

Strategic marketing plans to help you grow audience and sales, plus support for crowdfunding campaigns. Email lists, Amazon optimization, Kickstarter support, and more!
